💡 HOW DEAD MAN'S SWITCH WORKS

Unlike active monitors that probe your server, SwitchGuard is passive and zero-attack-surface: your cron job or background script simply pings its unique URL after execution. If SwitchGuard doesn't receive a ping before the deadline + grace period expires, it assumes the task crashed or stalled and immediately triggers the alarm.
